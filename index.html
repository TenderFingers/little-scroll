Build flash cards opens the iPhone photo picker, puts what you choose into a new album, then asks you to say and type the name of each photo.

Before you choose photos

After you tap Add in the iPhone picker, the iPhone gets each photo ready before this app can see it. You may see nothing happen for several seconds, and longer for many photos or photos stored in iCloud. Tap Add only once. Choosing 10 to 20 at a time is quicker.
